# Security Policy

## Supported versions

This repository is the public marketing site for [Voltaic Hosting](https://voltaic.host). Security fixes apply to the latest commit on the default branch.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **team@voltaic.host** with:

- A short description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Impact if you have assessed it
- Any proof-of-concept that stays non-destructive

Do not open a public GitHub issue for security reports.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days. Coordinated disclosure is preferred. Please give us a reasonable window to patch before publishing details.

## Scope

In scope:

- This website and its deployment configuration in this repository
- Accidental exposure of secrets, credentials, or private customer data through this site

Out of scope:

- Denial of service against production hosts
- Social engineering of staff or customers
- Findings that require physical access or compromised end-user devices
- Third-party services we do not operate

## Safe harbor

If you follow this policy, act in good faith, and avoid privacy violations or service disruption, we will not pursue legal action for research reported through the channel above.
